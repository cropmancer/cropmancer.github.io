CROPMANCER

The idle farm that grows while you sleep — a browser idle-farming game on Solana, powered by the $MANCER token and the Genesis Plot NFT.
Status: pre-launch. This document describes the game's design. Nothing in it is a promise of price, earnings, or return.

1. Introduction

Cropmancer is an idle farming game. Your farm produces on its own — while you work, while you sleep, while the app is closed. You check in once or twice a day, claim your harvest, sell it for $MANCER, and spend $MANCER on upgrades that make your farm produce faster. No reflexes. No grinding. Just numbers going up, on-chain.

2. Getting in — the Genesis Plot NFT

Entry is a one-time $1 registration, paid in SOL. In return, the player receives a Genesis Plot NFT:

  • It is the player's access key — the game checks NFT ownership from the connected wallet. No NFT, no farm.
  • It is the player's starter land — every farm begins as one Genesis Plot.
  • It is a compressed NFT (cNFT) on Solana, so minting costs a fraction of a cent and nearly all of the $1 funds the game economy.
  • It is transferable — a player who stops playing can sell their plot to a new player on any Solana NFT marketplace.

One active farm per Genesis Plot NFT.

3. The game loop

  1. Produce — your farm generates Produce automatically, 24/7, at your production rate.
  2. Claim — open the game and claim the accumulated harvest. Offline production is capped at 12 hours, so checking in twice a day is optimal — never mandatory.
  3. Sell — sell Produce for $MANCER in the in-game Harvest Market. The Produce→$MANCER rate is set by the Rewards Pool (Section 5), not fixed, so rewards stay sustainable.
  4. Upgrade — spend $MANCER on land expansions, animals, and machines. Every upgrade raises your production rate. A portion of every upgrade spend is burned.
  5. Compete — a weekly production leaderboard ranks every farm. Seasons reset the race, not your farm.

Production is calculated server-side from timestamps. Changing your device clock or editing the page does nothing — the server is the referee.

Utility — $MANCER is the game's only currency:

  • Earn it by selling your harvest in the Harvest Market.
  • Spend it on upgrades: land, animals, machines, and future cosmetics and season passes.
  • Burn it — 10% of all upgrade spending is burned forever, so the more people play, the scarcer $MANCER gets.

5. The economy — sources and sinks

The economy is designed as a loop, not a faucet.

Sources of $MANCER rewards. The Rewards Pool pays for harvests. It is funded by 50% of every $1 registration and by creator fees from $MANCER trading (0.30% of bonding-curve volume on pump.fun; tiered after graduation). The Produce→$MANCER rate floats with the pool's balance: a full pool pays generously, a thin pool pays less. Rewards can never exceed what the pool actually holds.

Sinks. Upgrades (90% recycled to the Rewards Pool, 10% burned). Future: season passes, cosmetics, extra plots.

Project revenue. 50% of registration fees, plus a creator-fee share allocated to operations.

This structure means player rewards come from new players and trading activity — stated plainly, the way it should be.

6. Fair play

  • Production is computed server-side; client tampering has no effect.
  • One farm per Genesis Plot NFT.
  • The Rewards Pool balance and the Produce→$MANCER rate are displayed in-game, in the open.

7. Risks and disclaimer

There is no guarantee of any price, earnings, or return. Game parameters in this litepaper may be tuned before launch; material changes will be announced on the official channels.
