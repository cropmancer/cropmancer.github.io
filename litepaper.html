CROPMANCER

The idle RPG that fights while you sleep — a browser idle hero-battler on Solana, powered by the $MANCER token and the Genesis Hero NFT.
Status: pre-launch. This document describes the game's design. Nothing in it is a promise of price, earnings, or return.

1. Introduction

Cropmancer is an idle RPG. You are the Cropmancer — a summoner. Your hero party battles on its own: while you work, while you sleep, while the app is closed. You check in once or twice a day, claim the loot your heroes won, sell it for $MANCER, and spend $MANCER on gear, skills, and new heroes that make your party stronger. Like a classic MMO RPG, your account has a level: battles earn EXP, and leveling up unlocks harder zones, extra hero slots, and richer loot. No reflexes. No grinding. Just heroes fighting and numbers going up, on-chain.

2. Getting in — the Genesis Hero NFT

Entry is a one-time $1 registration, paid in SOL. In return, the player receives a Genesis Hero NFT:

  • It is the player's access key — the game checks NFT ownership from the connected wallet. No NFT, no party.
  • It is the player's first hero — the Cropmancer summoner himself; every account begins with him.
  • It is an NFT on Solana, minted to the paying wallet when the $1 payment lands. Minting costs a small fraction of the $1 (network rent + fees); the rest funds the game economy.
  • It is transferable — a player who stops playing can sell their hero (and account access) to a new player on any Solana NFT marketplace.

One active account per Genesis Hero NFT.

3. The game loop

  1. Battle — your heroes auto-battle waves of monsters automatically, 24/7, earning Loot and EXP at your party's power rate.
  2. Claim — open the game and claim the accumulated loot. Offline earnings are capped at 12 hours, so checking in twice a day is optimal — never mandatory.
  3. Sell — sell Loot for $MANCER in the in-game Market. The Loot→$MANCER rate is set by the Rewards Pool (Section 5), not fixed, so rewards stay sustainable.
  4. Upgrade — spend $MANCER on hero gear, skills, and hero levels. Every upgrade raises your party's power. A portion of every upgrade spend is burned.
  5. Level up — battles earn EXP and your account level rises, MMO-style: higher account levels unlock harder zones, extra hero slots, and higher loot rates.
  6. Compete — a leaderboard ranks every account by power and loot. Seasons reset the race, not your heroes. Leaderboard rewards: the Top 500 split a 10,000 USDT reward pool, paid in SOL, funded by the Rewards Pool — the higher the rank, the bigger the share.

Battles are calculated server-side from timestamps. Changing your device clock or editing the page does nothing — the server is the referee.

5. The economy — sources and sinks

The economy is designed as a loop, not a faucet.

Sources of $MANCER rewards. The Rewards Pool pays for loot. It is funded by 50% of every $1 registration and by creator fees from $MANCER trading (0.30% of bonding-curve volume on pump.fun; tiered after graduation). The Loot→$MANCER rate floats with the pool's balance: a full pool pays generously, a thin pool pays less. Rewards can never exceed what the pool actually holds.

Sinks. Hero upgrades (90% recycled to the Rewards Pool, 10% burned). Future: season passes, cosmetics, extra heroes.

Project revenue. 50% of registration fees, plus a creator-fee share allocated to operations.

This structure means player rewards come from new players and trading activity — stated plainly, the way it should be.

6. Fair play

  • Battles are computed server-side; client tampering has no effect.
  • One account per Genesis Hero NFT.
  • The Rewards Pool balance and the Loot→$MANCER rate are displayed in-game, in the open.
