An economy that loops

Player rewards are paid from a Rewards Pool — funded by 50% of every registration plus creator fees from $MANCER trading. The Produce→$MANCER rate floats with the pool, so rewards never exceed what the pool holds. Upgrades recycle 90% back to the pool and burn 10% forever: the more people play, the scarcer $MANCER gets. Production is calculated server-side — the server is the referee, clock tricks do nothing.

Questions, answered

When does Cropmancer launch?

Registration (the Genesis Plot NFT mint) is open now. The $MANCER token listing and the farm opening come next, with no fixed date yet; watch @MuseAIv3 on X for the announcement.

How do I register?

On the register page: send exactly 0.0145 SOL (≈$1) from a wallet you control to the address shown there. Our system detects the payment and mints your Genesis Plot NFT to that same wallet, usually within about 10 minutes. Never pay from an exchange — the NFT would go to the exchange's wallet.

What do I get for the $1?

One Genesis Plot NFT: your access key and starter land in one. It is a compressed NFT on Solana, so minting costs a fraction of a cent. Pay once, keep the plot — there is no subscription.

How do I earn $MANCER?

Your farm produces automatically. Claim your Produce and sell it in the in-game Harvest Market for $MANCER. The exchange rate floats with the Rewards Pool, so payouts stay within what the pool holds.

Can I sell my Genesis Plot NFT?

Yes — it is transferable. If you stop playing, you can sell your plot to another player on any Solana NFT marketplace. The buyer takes over that farm's land.

Do I have to play every day?

No. Offline harvest piles up for up to 12 hours, so checking in once or twice a day is enough. Production is calculated server-side, so changing your device clock does nothing.

Is $MANCER an investment?

No. It is a game token: its price is set by traders and can go to zero. Registration buys a game access NFT, not an investment, and there is no guarantee of any price, earnings, or return.
